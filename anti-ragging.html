Anti-Ragging Policy & Safety

Hopewell Group of Colleges strictly enforces UGC, INC, and Government of Rajasthan anti-ragging regulations across all nursing, pharmacy, and Ayurvedic healthcare departments.

24/7 National Emergency

National Anti-Ragging Helpline

Free, confidential assistance for students nationwide. Direct UGC Monitoring Portal.

Call Toll-Free: 1800-180-5522
Email: antiragging@yahoo.com

UGC & INC Guidelines

Ragging in any form — physical, verbal, mental, or psychological — is strictly prohibited inside college premises, hostel blocks, and transport vehicles.

Anti-Ragging Squad

Special faculty surveillance squads conduct surprise inspections at hostels, library halls, and campus common areas to guarantee complete student safety.

Mandatory Affidavit

Every admitted student and their parents/guardians must submit an official anti-ragging undertaking affidavit online at antiragging.in upon admission.

Consequences of Ragging Violations

Any student found guilty of ragging will be subject to strict disciplinary actions including:

Immediate Rustication / Expulsion
Cancellation of Admission & Degree
Filing of Police FIR (IPC Offense)
Debarment from Examinations
