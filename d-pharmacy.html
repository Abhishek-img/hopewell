D.Pharmacy Program Overview & Learning Outcomes

The Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharmacy) is a foundational 2-year technical program designed under the ER-2020 Education Regulations of the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). The course equips students with comprehensive expertise in drug formulation, pharmaceutical chemistry, pharmacology, dosage calculations, and community pharmacy management.

At Hopewell College of Pharmacy, students gain hands-on laboratory practical experience combined with a mandatory 500 hours of clinical hospital pharmacy training across government and multi-specialty hospitals. Graduates become eligible for immediate registration as Registered Pharmacists (RPh) with state pharmacy councils.

Eligibility & Admission Criteria

1
Educational Qualification: Passed 10+2 / Senior Secondary Examination from a recognized Board (RBSE / CBSE / ICSE or equivalent).
2
Mandatory Science Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) OR Mathematics (PCM) with minimum 45% aggregate (40% for reserved categories).
3
Age Limit: Minimum 17 years of age on or before December 31 of the admission year.
4
Career Qualification: Eligible to apply for State Registered Pharmacist (RPh) badge and Drug Retail/Wholesale License.

2-Year PCI ER-2020 Course Curriculum & Subjects

Our curriculum follows the updated Pharmacy Council of India (ER-2020) standards, delivering balanced theoretical modules and extensive practical laboratory classes.

Y1

First Year Curriculum

Foundational Pharmaceutical Sciences & Labs
5 Theory + 5 Practical Labs
Pharmaceutics - Theory & Practical
Formulation of liquid dosages, powders, tablets, ointments, and packaging technology.
Pharmaceutical Chemistry - Theory & Practical
Structure, chemical properties, synthesis, purity testing, and volumetric analysis.
Pharmacognosy - Theory & Practical
Study of crude drugs from natural sources, medicinal plants, alkaloids, and phytochemistry.
Human Anatomy & Physiology
Detailed biological organ systems, cellular physiology, and hematology tests.
Social Pharmacy
Public health, preventative medicine, nutrition, national health programs, and OTC drugs.
Y2

Second Year Curriculum

Advanced Pharmacology & Clinical Practice
6 Theory + 5 Practical Labs
Pharmacology - Theory & Practical
Drug actions, mechanisms of drug absorption, therapeutic effects, and adverse drug reactions.
Community Pharmacy & Management
Retail shop setup, inventory control, prescription handling, and patient drug counseling.
Biochemistry & Clinical Pathology
Enzymes, metabolic pathways, blood glucose testing, lipid profiles, and urine diagnostic tests.
Pharmacotherapeutics & Hospital Pharmacy
Clinical treatment guidelines, hospital drug supply, unit dose dispensing, and sterile preparation.
Pharmacy Law & Ethics
Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940, Pharmacy Act 1948, NDPS Act, and code of pharmaceutical ethics.

State-of-the-Art PCI Approved Pharmacy Laboratories

Our campus features dedicated laboratories equipped with modern analytical equipment, tablet preparation machinery, distillation units, and digital dispensing systems.

Pharmaceutics Lab

Career Scope & Professional Opportunities for D.Pharm Graduates

D.Pharmacy graduates hold statutory qualifications allowing them to enter immediate employment or launch profitable healthcare businesses.

Registered Retail Pharmacist

Obtain a Retail Drug License to open and manage your own retail pharmacy shop or medical agency.

Hospital Pharmacist

Work in Govt. Hospitals (AIIMS, State Medical Health Services) and corporate multi-specialty hospitals managing drug distribution.

Medical Representative (MR)

Represent top multinational pharmaceutical companies (Sun Pharma, Cipla, Mankind, Lupin) in sales & clinical promotion.

Quality Control Associate

Conduct quality assurance, batch testing, and production packaging oversight in pharmaceutical manufacturing units.
