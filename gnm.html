GNM Course Overview & Scope

General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) is a comprehensive 3-year diploma program designed to produce competent frontline healthcare professionals. The program covers basic anatomy, physiology, microbiology, pharmacology, medical-surgical nursing, pediatrics, mental health, and midwifery.

At Hopewell Colleges, GNM students undergo intensive clinical postings in multi-specialty hospitals across Alwar. Students practice bedside patient monitoring, wound dressing, medication administration, maternal delivery care, and community healthcare drives.

Council Registrations & Licensing

Fully recognized by the Rajasthan Nursing Council (RNC), Jaipur and Indian Nursing Council (INC), New Delhi. Upon course completion, graduates register as Registered Nurse & Registered Midwife (RN & RM).

GNM Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification: 10+2 passed from a recognized educational board in any stream (Science, Arts, or Commerce).
Minimum Qualifying Marks: Minimum 40% aggregate marks in 10+2.
Age Criteria: Minimum 17 years completed as of 31st December of the admission year.
Gender Eligibility: Open for both male and female candidates.

Career Pathways & Job Opportunities

GNM diploma holders enjoy immediate employability across government health departments, private multi-specialty hospitals, and community health centers.

Staff Nurse / Ward Sister

Managing general patient wards, ICU beds, emergency trauma care, and assisting doctors in corporate hospitals.

Community Health Officer (CHO)

Serving in Government Primary Health Centers (PHCs), sub-centers, and rural health missions across Rajasthan.

Maternal & Child Nurse

Specialized midwife care in maternity homes, labor rooms, pediatric wards, and neonatal ICUs (NICU).

Post-Basic B.Sc Nursing Entry

Eligible to pursue 2-Year Post-Basic B.Sc Nursing degree to elevate career status to M.Sc & Nursing Superintendent.
