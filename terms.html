Terms & Conditions

Official terms governing student admissions, fee structures, academic attendance, hospital clinical postings, and campus rules at Hopewell Colleges Alwar.

1. Admission Eligibility & Document Verification

Admissions to B.Sc Nursing, GNM, D.Pharmacy, and DAN&P courses are granted strictly on merit and subject to eligibility criteria prescribed by RUHS, Indian Nursing Council (INC), Rajasthan Nursing Council (RNC), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), and DSRRAU Jodhpur.

All submitted marksheets, domicile certificates, caste certificates, and medical fitness certificates must be authentic. Provision of false or fabricated documents will lead to immediate cancellation of admission.

2. Fee Payment & Refund Guidelines

Tution fees, examination fees, library deposits, and hostel fees must be deposited as per the payment schedule notified by the accounts department.

  • Fee refunds, if requested prior to session commencement, follow statutory RUHS/INC refund norms.
  • No fee refund will be issued after the last date of university admission cutoff.

3. Attendance & Clinical Duty Regulations

As mandated by regulatory councils, students must maintain minimum 80% attendance in theory lectures and 100% attendance in practical hospital clinical duties to be eligible for university annual examinations.

4. Campus Code of Conduct

Students are expected to maintain professional decorum, wear official uniforms during campus & hospital hours, and respect college property. Substance abuse, smoking, or unauthorized absences are strictly prohibited.

5. Legal Jurisdiction

All legal disputes or claims arising out of college admissions, academic activities, or examinations are subject exclusively to the jurisdiction of courts in Alwar, Rajasthan.
