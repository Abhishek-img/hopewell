DAN&P (Diploma in Ayurved Nursing and Pharmacy)

DAN&P is a 2.6-year specialized diploma course providing theoretical knowledge and practical training in Ayurvedic medicine, herbal pharmacognosy, Bhasma/Rasayana preparation, Panchakarma care, and clinical nursing.

DAN&P Course Overview

The Diploma in Ayurved Nursing and Pharmacy (DAN&P) is a unique program blending ancient Indian medical wisdom with modern healthcare standards. Students learn Ayurvedic Sharir Kriya, Dravyaguna (Herbal Pharmacology), Rasa Shastra (Medicinal Alchemy), Bhaishajya Kalpana (Formulation Science), and Panchakarma nursing care.

Students receive hands-on training in herbal identification in our campus herbal garden and practical clinical rotations at affiliated Ayurvedic hospitals and research institutions.

Affiliation & University Authorization

  • Rajasthan Ayurved Nursing Council, Jaipur: State regulatory approval.
  • DSRRAU Jodhpur: Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Rajasthan Ayurved University, Jodhpur.

DAN&P Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification: 10+2 Passed in any recognized stream.
Exact Course Duration: 2.6 Years Diploma Course.
Career Roles: Ayurvedic Hospital Staff Nurse, Panchakarma Therapist, Herbal Pharmacist, Govt. Ayurved Hospital Nurse.
