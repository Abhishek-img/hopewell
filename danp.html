DAN&P Program Overview & Holistic Health Training

The Diploma in Ayurved Nursing and Pharmacy (DAN&P) is a specialized 2.6-year professional program authorized by Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Rajasthan Ayurved University (DSRRAU Jodhpur) and recognized by the Rajasthan Ayurved Nursing Council.

This program offers dual expertise in Ayurvedic Nursing Care and Herbal Pharmacy Formulation. Students gain practical mastery over Panchakarma therapies (Abhyanga, Shirodhara, Swedana), Ayurvedic pharmacology (Dravyaguna), herbal medicine preparation (Bhaishajya Kalpana), and clinical hospital management.

1. Ayurvedic Nursing Care

Panchakarma assistance, hospital ward care, pulse diagnosis (Nadi Pariksha) support, and Ayurvedic surgical (Shalya) nursing.

2. Herbal Pharmacy Science

Cultivation of medicinal plants, extraction of Kwath/Asava/Arishta, Churna formulation, and herbal shop management.

DAN&P Eligibility Criteria

1
Educational Qualification: Passed 10+2 / Senior Secondary Examination in Science, Arts, or Commerce stream from a recognized board.
2
Course Breakdown: 2 Years Academic Classroom & Practical Training + 6 Months Mandatory Hospital Clinical Internship.
3
Affiliating Body: Affiliated with Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan Rajasthan Ayurved University (DSRRAU Jodhpur).
4
Council Registration: Eligible for registration with Rajasthan Ayurved Nursing Council upon completion.

DAN&P Academic & Practical Course Curriculum

The curriculum prescribed by DSRRAU Jodhpur balances foundational Ayurvedic medicine theory with practical clinical care and herbal laboratory formulation.

Y1

First Year Curriculum

Foundational Ayurvedic Sciences
Theory & Practical Labs
Fundamentals of Ayurved (Ayurved Siddhanta)
Tridosha theory (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), Sapta Dhatu, Mala, Agni, and basic principles of healing.
Sharir Rachna & Sharir Kriya (Anatomy & Physiology)
Human body organ systems, Srotas (channels), Marma points, biological mechanisms, and vital signs.
Dravyaguna Vigyan (Herbal Pharmacology)
Study of medicinal plants, Rasa, Guna, Veerya, Vipaka, Prabhava, and herb identification.
Swasthavritta, Yoga & Hygiene
Personal hygiene, Dinacharya (daily routine), Ritucharya (seasonal routine), Yogic exercises, and dietetics.
Y2

Second Year & 6-Month Internship

Pharmacy Formulation & Clinical Care
Clinical & Hospital Rotation
Rasa Shastra & Bhaishajya Kalpana
Preparation of herbal extracts, Asava, Arishta, Churna, Vati, Gutta, and medicinal oil (Sneha Paka).
Panchakarma & Ayurvedic Nursing Care
Assisting Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, Shirodhara therapies, and pre/post treatment nursing care.
Rog Vigyan & Kayachikitsa Nursing
Ayurvedic clinical nursing for general illnesses, surgical nursing (Shalya Tantra), and wound dressing (Vrana Bandhana).
6 Months Mandatory Hospital Internship
Hands-on hospital rotations in Ayurvedic IPD/OPD wards, Panchakarma centers, and pharmacy dispensing units.

Career Opportunities for DAN&P Diploma Holders

With the rapid global growth of Ayurveda, Yoga, and Naturopathy (AYUSH), DAN&P graduates have diverse, highly lucrative job avenues.

Ayurvedic Hospital Nurse

Appointment as Staff Nurse in Government AYUSH Hospitals, State Ayurvedic Dispensaries, and private hospitals.

Panchakarma Therapist

Work as certified Panchakarma specialist in premium wellness resorts, AYUSH wellness centers, and international spas.

Herbal Pharmacist & Store Manager

Manage Ayurvedic pharmacies, government drug store dispensaries, or open self-owned Ayurvedic medical retail agencies.

Ayurvedic Pharma Production Chemist

Work in manufacturing plants (Dabur, Baidyanath, Patanjali, Himalaya) in raw herb testing, production, and quality control.
