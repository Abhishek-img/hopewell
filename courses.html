Our Professional Courses

Explore our INC, RNC, RUHS, PCI and RANC approved degree & diploma programs designed for high-impact healthcare careers.

4 Years Degree Program

B.Sc. Nursing (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)

A comprehensive 4-year undergraduate professional degree covering medical-surgical nursing, maternal health, child nursing, mental health, and community nursing services.

Affiliation: RUHS Jaipur
Recognition: RNC & INC New Delhi
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology & English (Min 45%)
View Details
3 Years Diploma Program

GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery)

A 3-year diploma program empowering students with practical bedside patient care skills, midwifery practice, infection control, and primary healthcare delivery.

Recognition: RNC & INC New Delhi
Eligibility: 10+2 in Science, Arts or Commerce (Min 40% aggregate)
Career Roles: Staff Nurse, Ward Supervisor, Community Health Nurse
View Details
2 Years Diploma Program

D.Pharmacy (Diploma in Pharmacy)

A 2-year pharmaceutical diploma designed to provide core knowledge in pharmacology, drug formulation, hospital pharmacy, and retail pharmacy operation.

Affiliation: RUHS Jaipur & PCI New Delhi
Approval: Rajasthan State Pharmacy Council
Eligibility: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Maths
View Details
2.6 Years Diploma Program

DAN&P (Diploma in Ayurved Nursing & Pharmacy)

A specialized 2.6-year diploma program integrating traditional Ayurvedic care, herbal pharmacy preparation, Panchakarma therapies, and modern clinical nursing principles.

Affiliation: RANC, Rajasthan
Admission Process: Government Counselling by RANC ,Rajasthan
Eligibility: 10+2 any Stream (Min 17 Yrs, Max 25 Yrs)
View Details
