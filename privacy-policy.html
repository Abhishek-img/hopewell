Privacy Policy

Hopewell Group of Colleges respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, protect, and use student application data across our websites and campus portals.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out online admission forms, request campus tour callbacks, or register for counseling sessions, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: Name, Date of Birth, Father/Guardian Name.
  • Contact details: Phone number, WhatsApp number, Email address, Residential address.
  • Academic credentials: Class 10th & 12th marks, PCB percentage, entrance exam scores.
  • Course preferences: B.Sc Nursing, GNM, D.Pharmacy, or DAN&P Ayurved.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is strictly used for official educational and administrative purposes, including:

  • Processing course admission applications and verifying eligibility.
  • Communicating merit list updates, RUHS/RNC/PCI counseling notifications, and fee schedules.
  • Scheduling personal campus guided tours and faculty counseling sessions.
  • Maintaining mandatory university and state nursing council registration records.

3. Data Confidentiality & Protection

Hopewell Educational Group enforces administrative and technical security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We do NOT sell, trade, or rent student contact information to third-party commercial marketing agencies.

4. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our official website uses standard browser cookies and aggregated analytics tools to enhance page load performance and optimize website navigation. You may choose to disable cookies in your browser settings if preferred.
