Lamp Lighting & Florence Nightingale Oath Ceremony

The Lamp Lighting ceremony is a milestone ritual for every novice nurse at Hopewell Colleges. Passing the flame from senior faculty to students symbolizes the transfer of knowledge, compassion, and commitment to human service.

Symbol of Compassion

Light from Florence Nightingale's lamp inspires dedicated healthcare service.

Ethical Nursing Pledge
