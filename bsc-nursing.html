B.Sc. Nursing (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)

B.Sc. Nursing is a 4-year undergraduate professional degree course designed to prepare students for professional nursing practice, patient-centered care, clinical management, and higher academic specialization.

Course Overview & Scope

The B.Sc. Nursing curriculum at Hopewell Colleges equips students with a strong foundation in biological sciences, anatomy, physiology, microbiology, pharmacology, and clinical nursing. Students gain comprehensive skills to assess patient conditions, administer specialized nursing care, operate medical equipment, and manage hospital wards.

Graduates are fully eligible for state nursing council registration and can pursue careers as Registered Nurses (RN), Military Nursing Officers, Clinical Tutors, ICU Specialists, and Nursing Administrators globally.

Official Affiliations & Recognitions

  • RUHS Jaipur: Affiliated with Rajasthan University of Health Sciences, Jaipur.
  • RNC Jaipur: Recognized by Rajasthan Nursing Council.
  • INC New Delhi: Approved by Indian Nursing Council, New Delhi.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifying Examination: Passed 10+2 / Senior Secondary Examination from a recognized board (CBSE/RBSE/ICSE).
Mandatory Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB) and English core/elective.
Minimum Marks: Minimum 45% aggregate in PCB for General candidates (40% for SC/ST/OBC).
Age Limit: Minimum 17 years completed as of 31st December of the admission year.

4-Year Course Structure

Year 1

Foundational Medical Sciences

  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Nutrition & Biochemistry
  • Nursing Foundation & First Aid
  • Psychology & Microbiology
  • English & Computer Basics
Year 2

Core Clinical Nursing

  • Medical-Surgical Nursing I
  • Pharmacology, Pathology, Genetics
  • Community Health Nursing I
  • Communication & Educational Technology
  • Clinical Ward Rotation (300+ hrs)
Year 3

Specialized Patient Care

  • Medical-Surgical Nursing II
  • Child Health Nursing (Pediatrics)
  • Mental Health Nursing (Psychiatry)
  • Nursing Research & Statistics
  • Supervised Specialty Hospital Clinicals
Year 4

Advanced Practice & Internship

  • Midwifery & Obstetrical Nursing
  • Community Health Nursing II
  • Management of Nursing Services
  • Compulsory Rotational Clinical Internship

Career Opportunities for B.Sc Nurses

Staff Nurse / ICU Nurse

Government and private multi-specialty hospitals, ICUs, and critical care units.

Government Nursing Officer

AIIMS NORCET, Military Nursing Service (MNS), Railway Hospitals, and State PSC roles.

Clinical Tutor / Educator

Teaching faculty in nursing colleges and clinical training centers after M.Sc Nursing.

Global Nursing Opportunities

High demand in NHS UK, NCLEX-RN USA, Middle East, Germany & Canada.
